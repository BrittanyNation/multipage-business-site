About PureNest Cleaning

PureNest started — in this fictional story — with a simple frustration: cleaning services that were either hard to book, vague on price, or inconsistent from visit to visit.

So PureNest was built around three promises: clear pricing before we arrive, the same checklist every visit, and friendly communication before, during, and after each clean. The brand is intentionally calm and fresh — mint and white, plain language, no pressure — because hiring help for your home should feel easy, not salesy.

Our values

Consistency

Checklists, notes on your preferences, and quality checks so visit ten is as good as visit one.

Respect for your home

Shoes covered, pets respected, fragile items handled with care, and doors locked on departure.

Clear communication

Confirmations, arrival windows, and honest updates if anything changes — no surprises.

Meet the team (generic)

Team members are presented generically with initials avatars — no fake-person photos — as a responsible pattern for portfolio and early-stage client sites.

TL

Team Lead

Trains cleaners, runs quality checks, and is the point of contact for recurring clients.

CS

Cleaning Specialist

Deep-clean and move-out expert, focused on kitchens, bathrooms, and detail work.

CO

Client Coordinator

Handles booking, scheduling, and follow-up so clients always know what's next.

Trust & safety

  • Cleaners presented as background-checked and insured in this concept's trust copy
  • Supplies included, with product preferences noted per household
  • Satisfaction promise: report an issue within 24 hours for a re-clean of the area
  • Keys / entry codes handled via a documented, client-approved method only
